Your son can use the Food & Photo Analyzer tab to either search for a dish and adjust the gram portion or upload a photo of his plate (or test one of the sample gym meal presets) to see its protein, calories, carbohydrates, healthy fats, vitamins (A, B6, B12, C, D, Folate), and minerals (Calcium, Iron, Magnesium, Zinc, Potassium, Phosphorus). The Protein Food Guide tab lets you filter and compare high-protein foods across Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Eggs & Dairy, Plant-Based/Vegan, and Quick Gym Snack categories—and includes an Add Family Food Suggestion button so you can add your own favorite homemade dishes to the list. Finally, he can click Edit Athlete Profile at the top to set his age, weight, and workout frequency, then log meals throughout the day in the Daily Gym Log to track his progress and get personalized food combinations to hit his daily protein target.
