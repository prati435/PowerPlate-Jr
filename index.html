Why Vitamins & Minerals Matter Alongside Protein

Protein builds muscle fibers after the gym, but growing kids and teens need key vitamins and minerals to absorb that protein, strengthen growing bones, carry oxygen to muscles, and prevent cramps.

🏋️ Set Kid / Teen Athlete Profile

💡 Safe Youth Nutrition Note: Active teens generally need 1.2g to 1.6g of protein per kg of body weight from whole foods (milk, paneer, eggs, dal, chicken, sprouts, nuts) rather than heavy adult supplements.
